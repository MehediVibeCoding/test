INSTALLATION GUIDE

How to Install a 5 Meter RGB Neon Light

A dedicated guide captures installation-related searches without turning the product page into a wall of text.

01

Plan the design

Decide the route before mounting. Light pencil marks can help you keep the final pattern accurate.

02

Use two people if possible

One person positions the light while the other secures mounting points.

03

Choose mounting method

For straight layouts, use the supplied plastic clips. For custom designs, a thin 1-inch nail can be a practical option based on the product experience provided.

04

Avoid glue

Adhesive/glue is not recommended because the light's construction and weight can make adhesive mounting unreliable.

05

Avoid ceiling & tin walls

Ceiling mounting is not recommended because improper mounting can create a falling hazard. Tin walls are also not recommended.

06

Connect power

Wall AC → 24V 1A adapter → DC connector → inline switch → neon light.
